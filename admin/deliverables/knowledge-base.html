Multi-Agent SDLC System — Knowledge Base

18 agents. 9 gates. One repo that builds and governs itself.

This platform builds AI-centric applications for the energy industry through a gated, human-approved pipeline — and evolves its own agent roster through the same kind of governed process it applies to every project. Click a gate below to see who acts there.

Intake

Runs unconditionally, before Team Composition even decides who stays on the roster — this is deliberate: it avoids a circular dependency where the roster decision needs answers that would otherwise come from agents the roster hasn't approved yet.

Templates

Three shapes to start from

plan-agent picks between these based on your request — a document-grounded Q&A app, a conversational app, and a tool-using automation, are three genuinely different shapes, not variations of one.

FastAPI · LangGraph · API-only

agentic-workflow

A multi-step, tool-using agent invoked programmatically. No chat UI, no document corpus. Good fit for backend automation — "given a region, look up its status and decide whether to raise an alert."

Next.js · FastAPI · LangChain

genai-chatbot

A conversational, chat-first application, optionally with tool use. No document corpus to ground answers in — that's rag-knowledge-base's job.

Next.js · FastAPI · LangChain · Chroma

rag-knowledge-base

A question-answering app grounded in a real document corpus — answers must be retrieved from and cited against source documents, not just conversed about generically.

Getting Started

Build your first project

Start a project

Describe what you want in one line. If it's genuinely ambiguous between two templates, the platform asks — it doesn't guess.

/new-project "a Q&A tool grounded in our maintenance manuals"

Answer Intake, then trim the team

Domain and industry questions come first, unconditionally. Then decide which optional SMEs are worth the token cost — usage-monitor gives you a real number to weigh that against.

Approve each gate as it comes

Plan, Architecture, Code, Test, Review, Deploy — every one pauses for your sign-off. Nothing merges or ships silently.

Ship it, then keep going

Add features without waiting for a whole new project. Bundle finished features into a release when you're ready.

/enhance-project · /cut-release

Ask a specialist anytime

Don't wait for the next gate for a targeted question.
