What can it build?

Three kinds of application, each delivered through the same nine gates.

Support chatbots

Your customers ask in plain language; the chatbot answers only what it actually knows. You approve its tone, its limits, and its launch.

Automated workflows

Multi-step processes run by agents — with the judgment calls routed to a person, by design, not as an afterthought.

Knowledge assistants

Your documents, searchable by meaning, every answer citing its source. You approve what goes in and what stays out.

Concept 1's question chain is the spine — five pages, each answering its title in the first sentence. Concept 5's replay is the entire body of "/how": the pipeline is never described abstractly, only shown building a customer-support chatbot.

Three depths

Depth 0: read five sentences, understand everything. Depth 1: the replay stepper. Depth 2: closed-by-default expanders (full artifacts, roster detail, claims-to-source). The simple surface never shows them until asked.
