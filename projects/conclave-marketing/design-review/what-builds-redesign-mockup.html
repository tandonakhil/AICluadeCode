What can it build?

Not three templates — a general-purpose way to build the software your business runs on. Conclave's 16 specialist agents and 9 governance gates aren't tied to one kind of app.

The three starter templates just get the most common projects moving fast. The same governed build process — plan, design, secure, code, test, ship — produces a wide range of applications, each built and delivered into your own environment with a human in command at every gate.

Conclave builds web, API, data, and agentic software, delivered into your environment. Native mobile, embedded, and managed cloud hosting aren't part of the toolchain today — cloud delivery is on the roadmap.
