Why the gates exist at all

The gates exist so that a person decides, on the record, every time the work moves forward. That one choice is a deliberate answer to a well-documented pattern: 30% of generative-AI projects are abandoned after proof of concept (Gartner, 2024), and over 40% of agentic-AI projects are projected to be canceled by the end of 2027 (Gartner, 2025). Behind both numbers is the same failure — systems that advance without anyone deciding they should. Conclave makes the opposite bet: by design, nothing advances without an explicit human decision.
