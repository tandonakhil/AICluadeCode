What can it build?

Have an idea for software? Conclave builds it. Portals, internal tools, document and contract systems, analytics, agentic workflows — or something entirely your own. It’s a general-purpose way to build the software your business runs on, taking your idea from first sketch to production with a human in command at every gate.

Pure custom — your idea, built end to end

The templates are just the fast lane. Describe the software you actually need and the same governed pipeline builds it, idea to production. A few of the shapes it takes:

Contract & document intelligence

Extracts terms, flags obligations and renewal dates, and routes the exceptions to a person.

Why governed matters: contract calls carry legal weight — every extraction and routing is on the record.

Internal operations console

One dashboard for approvals, edits, and reporting — instead of scattered spreadsheets and admin panels.

Why governed matters: these tools write to live data — approval on any write step is a gate, not an afterthought.

Customer & partner portals

A branded portal where customers or partners check status, place orders, and pull their own data.

Why governed matters: portals expose internal data outward — the security gate scopes access before launch.

Decision-support & analytics

Surfaces cost and risk drivers and recommends the next move — with the reasoning shown.

Why governed matters: a recommendation that drives spend must be explainable — the trail captures why.

Integration & middleware

Why governed matters: integrations are where data quietly leaks — reviewed at each gate, they hold.

Agentic process automation

Why governed matters: most agent pilots die before production on governance — the gates are built for the survivors.
