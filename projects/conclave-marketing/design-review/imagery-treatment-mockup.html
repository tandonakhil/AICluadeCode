The designer's recommendation (and my agreement): a firm no to photographic AI stock art — no faces, teams, offices, robots, or glowing-brain clichés. On a site whose /why literally says "we won't invent a customer," fabricating people would be the most self-undermining thing we could add. Instead: 3 images, one treatment — "Pressed in paper" duotone macro material studies (drafting table, a wax seal, a shared worktable). Warmth comes from craft, paper grain, and warm light, not from depicting humans who don't exist.

How the treatment works: each image is generated grayscale; the Paper & Seal duotone is applied in CSS (shadows → ink/teal-ink, highlights → cream/paper), so one asset renders correctly in both themes — toggle the theme above to see it. Edges feather into the paper. Gold is never used (reserved for human-decision). Served as local WebP (<120KB each, ~360KB total) — never hotlinked, so the zero-external-requests guarantee holds. All decorative (alt=""), static (reduced-motion safe), no text over image.
