The problem this solves

Below is a slice of how /why reads today — a wall of prose. The devices above turn that same content into scannable, engaging units without adding a single word or a single stock image.

You stay in command of everything Conclave builds. So you shouldn't have to take its work on faith — you should be able to check it. You can: every claim on this site has a record behind it, including the failures, which we quote below unedited in substance. We won't show you a customer logo, because we don't have one yet — and we won't invent one. What we can show you is stronger. The site you're reading was built by Conclave, through its own nine gates, and the record of that build survives. The gates exist so that a person decides, on the record, every time the work moves forward… (continues for several more paragraphs).

What the research found (leading products in our category — Anthropic, Palantir, Linear, Stripe, Vercel): the leading way to engage users on serious/trust-led technical sites is not "add images" — it's rationed restraint + showing the product/system + breaking long text into visual units. Generic stock or AI photography actively hurts a trust brand (banner blindness + credibility red flag) — the single worst move for a product that sells honesty. So this is the plan:

P1 — editorial text-breaking kit (stat callouts, pull-quotes, split cards): highest impact, zero risk, directly fixes "too text-heavy." Applied to Home, /why, /what.
P2 — scroll-reveal the visuals we already own (Council Mark, /how replay, /who hierarchy, /what sample-UIs) so dense sections unfold on scroll instead of sitting as walls — fast (100–300ms), purposeful, reduced-motion-safe.
P3 — the honest "imagery": an on-brand, locally-generated abstract "council network" texture (shown up top) used sparingly as section backing/dividers — real imagery, generated from code, so it's self-contained AND can't read as fake stock. This is also why it needs no OpenAI API — nothing to fetch or fabricate.
P4 — a thin custom seal/line-icon spot-graphic set for section heads.

Deliberately avoided: stock/AI photos, human imagery, fake product screenshots, heavy 3D/scroll-jacking. This gives you more visual engagement AND far less text density — the two things you asked for — without a single move that would undercut the trust positioning.
