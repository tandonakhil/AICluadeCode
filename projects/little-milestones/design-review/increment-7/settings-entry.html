Increment 7 — F17 Google Photos import — Settings entry point

New "Google Photos" card in the existing .lm-settings-grid 2-up desktop layout (§10.5), same .lm-card anatomy as every other Settings card. Placed directly after the existing "Privacy" card (third-party account linking belongs in the privacy-adjacent group, not the family/digest group) — one new card, no new page. Scoped to the signed-in caregiver's own Google account, not the family or a specific child: any caregiver can connect their own Google Photos and import into any child profile they already have access to, exactly like any other memory-logging action under F10's existing caregiver permission model.

Third-party disclosure (INDUSTRY_KB §2.2): the "Not connected" copy below states plainly and up front that connecting hands Google an OAuth grant to this caregiver's Google account, that it is read-only, that it is off by default, and exactly what is (and is not) accessed — no library-wide access, picker-selection only, no ongoing sync. This is the disclosure surface; the review step (picker-review.html) repeats the private-storage reassurance at the point of import itself.
