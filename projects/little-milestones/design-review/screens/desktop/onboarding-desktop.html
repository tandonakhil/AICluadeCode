Onboarding is a short, linear, first-run form — not a place that benefits from reclaimed horizontal space. Widening it would only lengthen the eye's travel per line and dilute focus on one question at a time (§1.4's "one question per screen" rule). Decision: stays centered, narrow, max-width 440–480px, at every breakpoint.
