The 2am test — night colours. The warm-dark theme §1.3 specified as a fast-follow and light-only web shipped without. Warm near-black #211D1A, never pure black (OLED pure black at 2am is harsh, and the palette is warm by design). Slate keeps its role, deepened; terracotta shifts to peach for AA on dark. No white flash: the splash, modals and lightbox all honour the theme. “Automatic” follows the OS, with an explicit override for a parent whose phone is on Light but whose house is dark.

Hard-offline cold start. No session could be validated and no cache is warm. Two ways out, never a dead end: retry, or go straight to whatever is cached locally in a read-only mode. Copy names the app, never the protocol — no “Network request failed”, no host, no port, no status code (the calm-copy rule that already caught the web build out).

Accessibility contract

Every interactive element

has an accessibilityLabel that reads as a phrase a person would say (“Switch child, currently Maya”), an accessibilityRole, and a accessibilityHint only where the outcome isn’t obvious.

Touch targets

44×44pt minimum, everywhere, including the quiet underlined links — the visible label may be small, the hit area may not.

Contrast

AA on every token as used, in both light and night themes, including text over any photo-derived accent (UXR-13’s pre-render check ports unchanged — a failing check falls back to the fixed theme).

Reduce Motion

Disables the memory-settle animation, the lightbox zoom transition, and all skeleton shimmer. Screen transitions become cross-fades, never slides.

Screen reader on Journey

The timeline is traversable as a flat list of moments. The coral spine and chapter flourishes are accessibilityElementsHidden — decorative only, and carrying no meaning is what makes that safe (UXR-1).
