Today tab

The default landing tab. One job: in the first three seconds, tell a parent how old their child is today and give them something to actually do with them. Backed by GET /profiles/{id}/activities plus /digest and /products.

Order is deliberate: hero (who + how old) → activities (do-now) → what’s coming (invitation, never a deadline — UXR-8) → this week → ideas. Products sit last and always: a parent scrolls past everything useful before they see anything purchasable.
