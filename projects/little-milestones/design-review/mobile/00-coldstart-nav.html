Cold start & navigation architecture

The path from a cold launch to a selected child, and the tab set every other screen hangs off. Five states, left to right, in the order a parent meets them.

Navigation decision: bottom tabs (4 + one center capture action), each tab owning its own native stack. No drawer. Child switching is not a tab — it is the header chip on every tab, opening a bottom sheet, so switching child never costs a parent their place. Caregiver/account lives under You.

4 · Child switcher — a bottom sheet, thumb-reachable. Opened from the header chip on any tab; the sheet’s rows start at the bottom of the sheet stack so the most-used child is nearest the thumb. Why a centre capture button

The single most time-sensitive thing a parent does on a phone is record a moment that is happening right now. It gets the largest, most central, most reliably thumb-reachable target in the app — 56pt, one tap from anywhere, never buried in a tab.

Every tab: 44×44pt minimum, icon aria-hidden, label is the accessible name, selected tab exposes selected: true.
