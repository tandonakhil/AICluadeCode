What little-milestones is

Source: PLAN.md §1–§2, knowledge/INDUSTRY_KB.md §1

The pitch

little-milestones is a consumer parenting web app that turns a child's birthdate into an age- and corrected-age-aware milestone chat, a private photo & memory journal, and a multi-caregiver family space — explicitly not a healthcare or clinical product, and private-by-default with a child's photos and data.

Consumer parenting / family tech Chat-first, genai-chatbot template Non-clinical, non-medical

Target audience

Parents and caregivers of infants and toddlers (0–~3 years), in the same market segment as BabyCenter, Huckleberry, Wonder Weeks, and Qeepsake — direct-to-consumer, mobile/web-first, no clinical gatekeeping.

  • Primary user: a first-time or early-stage parent who wants reassurance and structure without a pediatric-grade tracker's clinical tone.
  • Household shape: multi-caregiver by design (F10) — the product assumes two or more adults share access to one child's space, not a single-user account.
  • Premature-birth families are explicitly designed for — corrected-age computation (F2) exists specifically because an app keyed only to birthdate systematically mis-ages preterm infants.

Value proposition

Age-true, not generic

Milestone chat and activity suggestions are conditioned on the child's real, corrected age — matching the market's shift from chat-only to predictive/personalized AI.

The memory keeper

Photo upload + a life-journey timeline is the emotional core proven to retain parents for years — long after sleep-tracking apps get abandoned.

Private by construction

No face processing, no AI training on child photos, encryption at rest, EXIF/location stripped on every upload — including photos imported from Google Photos.

Underlying technical architecture

Source: knowledge/ARCHITECTURE_KB.md §0–§12

Client API (FastAPI) First-party data External services

Frontend

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, fluid/adaptive CSS rebuilt from fixed breakpoints in Increment 5 after human review of a live mockup.

Backend

FastAPI + SQLite, a Store[T] interface convention, idempotent schema migrations on every startup.

Auth & security

Session-cookie auth, opt-in TOTP MFA, Fernet-encrypted secrets, argon2id recovery codes, rate-limited sensitive routes.

Photos

Every photo — direct upload or Google-imported — goes through one pipeline: sniff → EXIF-strip → encrypt → write. No parallel path.
