What little-milestones is

Source: PLAN.md §1–§2, knowledge/INDUSTRY_KB.md §1

The pitch

little-milestones is a consumer parenting web app that turns a child's birthdate into an age- and corrected-age-aware milestone chat, a private photo & memory journal, and a multi-caregiver family space — explicitly not a healthcare or clinical product, and explicitly private-by-default with a child's photos and data.

Consumer parenting / family tech Chat-first, genai-chatbot template Non-clinical, non-medical

Target audience

Parents and caregivers of infants and toddlers (0–~3 years), in the same market segment as BabyCenter, Huckleberry, Wonder Weeks, and Qeepsake — direct-to-consumer, mobile/web-first, no clinical gatekeeping.

  • Primary user: a first-time or early-stage parent who wants reassurance and structure without a pediatric-grade tracker's clinical tone.
  • Household shape: multi-caregiver by design (F10) — the product assumes two or more adults (parents, grandparents, nannies) share access to one child's space, not a single-user account.
  • Premature-birth families are explicitly designed for, not an afterthought — corrected-age computation (F2) exists specifically because an app keyed only to birthdate systematically mis-ages preterm infants (DOMAIN_KB R4).

Value proposition

Age-true, not generic

Milestone chat and activity suggestions are conditioned on the child's real, corrected age — not a one-size-fits-all script. Matches the 2026 market's shift from chat-only to predictive/personalized AI (Huckleberry SweetSpot, Sprouty's leap calendar).

The memory keeper

Photo upload + life-journey timeline is the emotional core proven to retain parents for years (Qeepsake, Lifecake precedent) — long after sleep-tracking apps get abandoned around 12–18 months.

Private by construction

No face processing, no AI training on child photos, encryption at rest, EXIF/location stripped on every upload — including photos imported from Google Photos. Trust is the product, not a footnote.

Why now (market framing)

The 2026 baby-tracker category has moved past chat-only AI toward tools that anticipate a child's stage and compound in value the more a family logs. Memory- keeping is the proven long-horizon retention engine in this category; multi- caregiver sharing extends value beyond a single parent. little-milestones is built around both, deliberately declining to lead with commerce or clinical claims.

Underlying technical architecture

Source: knowledge/ARCHITECTURE_KB.md §0–§12 (MVP through Increment 7 / Google Photos import)

Client (Next.js) API (FastAPI modules) First-party data (SQLite / encrypted store) External services

Frontend

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, fluid/adaptive CSS (clamp()/minmax()/ auto-fit) rebuilt from fixed breakpoints in Increment 5 after human review of a live mockup.

Backend

FastAPI + SQLite, a Store[T] interface convention across every domain object, idempotent schema migrations applied on every startup (safe against the already-populated dev DB).

Auth & security

Session-cookie auth, opt-in TOTP MFA with Fernet-encrypted secrets and argon2id-hashed recovery codes, password reset with full session invalidation, rate-limited sensitive routes.

Photos

Every photo — direct upload or Google-imported — goes through one pipeline: content-sniff → EXIF/GPS strip → Fernet encrypt → disk write. No parallel storage path exists for any source.

Structural guarantee: no photo ever reaches the LLM layer

photos.py / google_photos.py have zero import path into llm.py / prompts.py — verified by a static AST import-graph check at every Test gate, not just documented intent.
