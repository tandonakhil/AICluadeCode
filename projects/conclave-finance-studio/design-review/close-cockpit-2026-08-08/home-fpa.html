Refused — by design, permanently
We will not write the sentence that explains why the account moved.

A22. This product hands over the decomposition, the certified metrics, the joins and the population covered. It does not produce the causal narrative — “this rose because the storm programme accelerated” — because it cannot substantiate it, and a plausible sentence it cannot substantiate is worse than none in a document you sign.

This will not be built. It is not on a roadmap and it is not awaiting prioritisation.

What we refuse to do
Not in this release
Inquire mode, and an FP&A identity

Deferred, not refused. Planned. Inquire — asking a question of the certified layer and getting back the substantiable half of the answer — is designed and not built. F45, the flux surface, is deferred. Until they exist, this page is the whole of the product’s FP&A surface.

In this pilot there is no FP&A persona to sign in as: the build carries two, staff accountant and controller. This page is rendered against a third that does not yet exist, and that gap is a scope ruling rather than a design decision.

The two blocks above are deliberately adjacent and deliberately different: solid slate and filled is refused permanently; dashed outline with no fill is deferred. The distinction is carried in the wording as well as the styling, so it survives a greyscale print and a screen reader. A user who only ever saw one of the two treatments could not learn to tell them apart.

Why this page does not show you a KPI dashboard, in the system’s own words

Every figure this product computes for an FP&A analyst today — coverage, detections, dwell, override rate — is a fact about the close process, not about the accounts you are explaining. Rendering them here would give this page the density of the other two without giving it their usefulness, and the first time you acted on one it would be the wrong act.

Collapsed and last, deliberately, like every other narrative in this product.
