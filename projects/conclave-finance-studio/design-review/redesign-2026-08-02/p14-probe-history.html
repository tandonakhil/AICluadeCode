My probe history

Moved out of primary navigation, into the identity menu. Two reasons. It is per-person, private by design and empty most of the time, so it was spending a permanent top-level slot on a mostly-blank page. And more importantly: a product whose A24 refusal is “no agent may assess, score, rank or characterise a named human reviewer” should not advertise a per-person scoreboard in the same menu as the work. Reachable in one click from your own name, where a private thing belongs.
