Period record — 2026-05

This is a record of what was examined and what was concluded. It is not a statement that the period was clean.

This product does not conclude that a period is materially correct (A20) and does not certify a close (A21). Those are refused by design and will not be built. The question “was last month’s close clean?” has an honest answer here and it is not yes or no — it is the six figures below, including the ones that say nothing looked.

NEW. No retrospective, period-scoped surface exists anywhere in the pilot. Every screen is current-period only, so the controller journey “was last period’s close clean?” cannot be started, let alone finished. This is the redesign’s highest-value new screen and the one place where the product’s central refusal is rendered at the exact moment a user asks for the thing it refuses.

Precision is not the promotion gate. These labels come from reviewer acceptance, so they measure agreement with the reviewer, not correctness, and rise as reviewer scrutiny falls. A reviewer who accepts everything scores a skill at 1.0000. Readiness is computed from five conditions and from nothing else.
