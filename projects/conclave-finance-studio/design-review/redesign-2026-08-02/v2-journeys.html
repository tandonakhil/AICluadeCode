This journey depends on a scope decision that is not mine to make. It is drawn rather than omitted because you cannot rule on an FP&A surface you have not seen. The claim worth arguing: an inquiry surface that hands over the decomposition and openly refuses the narrative is more useful to an analyst who must defend a variance than one that manufactures a plausible sentence — and it is the only shape that does not breach A19 or A22.

What these four maps decide

J1 works but ends in silence. J2 and J3 cannot be walked. J4 has no persona. Three of the four failures are missing destinations, not missing features — the evidence, the controls and the grammar already exist and are good. That is the case for the information architecture, stated as journeys rather than as a menu.
