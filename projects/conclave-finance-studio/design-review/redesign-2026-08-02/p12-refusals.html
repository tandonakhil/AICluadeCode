What this product refuses to do

These are design properties of the product. They are not roadmap gaps and they will not be built. Kept reachable from the global nav — the gate-5 argument stands: burying a refusal makes it look like an omission, which is the exact failure the refusal grammar exists to prevent. Under Evidence is not a settings drawer.

A23 — an agent may never conclude that its own prior conclusion was correct

Self-consistency is not evidence of correctness. This is the management-judgement mechanism expressed as a refusal.

A24 — no agent may assess, score, rank or characterise a named human reviewer

The platform computes control metrics; no agent generates a natural-language judgement about a named person. This is what stops the product becoming a surveillance instrument by feature creep.

Where the product cannot substantiate a conclusion it declines to draw one, rather than drawing a weaker one and qualifying it in prose nobody reads.

DeferredNot in MVP1. Could exist later. Dashed border, amber — e.g. P1/P5 on agent readiness
