## Rendered-UI capture — a real browser against the SERVED pilot

Captured independently of the suite, because the suite renders through
`page.route()` over an in-process ASGI app. This capture drives **Chromium over
a real socket** against the pilot as found, which is what makes a screenshot
evidence of what a reader meets rather than of what a string contained.

Four screens × two viewports = **8 captures, 8 clean**. Both viewports are
desktop, which is **complete** rather than partial coverage for this project:
the recorded surface decision is desktop web only, so there is no responsive
requirement to under-serve.

Every capture asserted, from the browser's own resolved values:

* **compounded opacity** — the effective opacity of each text-bearing element
  multiplied up its whole ancestor chain, because a compounding-opacity defect
  exists only at this level and is invisible to any source-string check;
* **effectively invisible text** — anything whose compounded opacity is ≤ 0.01;
* **horizontal overflow** — `scrollWidth − clientWidth`;
* **stylesheet actually applied** — computed `body` background, not the
  presence of a `<style>` tag;
* **accessibility tree** — landmark roles, the level-1 heading, labelled
  controls;
* **console and page errors** during load.

Process lifecycle: the pilot was started, the browser launched, all eight
captures taken, the browser closed and the pilot reaped **inside one command
invocation**, ending with `8021 free — nothing left running`. Nothing survives
this turn.
