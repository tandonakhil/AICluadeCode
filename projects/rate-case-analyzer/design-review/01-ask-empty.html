What you are looking at

The screen an analyst sees on first load. Three deliberate choices:

  • No dashboard, no chrome, no illustration. One question field. This is the tool's whole job and the density arrives after you ask, never before.
  • The corpus freshness banner is in the app bar from the very first frame (AC-F39-01) — not on the answer screen only. If the ingestion job silently stopped, you learn it before you trust anything, not after.
  • The scope statement names what the corpus is and is not. Three commissions, twelve cases, base-rate proceedings only. Setting the boundary up front is what makes a later refusal read as expected rather than as breakage.

The example questions are real corpus-answerable questions, not marketing copy — they teach the shape of a question the frame parser can resolve (jurisdiction, parameter, period).

Ask a precedent question

Every answer is built from cited docket documents, or it is declined. Nothing is inferred, blended across jurisdictions, or averaged.

Name a commission, a parameter and a period. Unresolvable questions are declined rather than guessed at.

What this corpus contains. 12 curated base-rate proceedings across three commissions — Pennsylvania PUC, Public Utility Commission of Texas, and California PUC — each ingested with its outcome document (final order, approved settlement or compliance filing).

What it does not contain. Rider, tracker and formula-rate proceedings (excluded from base-rate treatment); data-request responses and hearing transcripts; scanned filings with no extractable text. These standing limits are restated on every answer.
