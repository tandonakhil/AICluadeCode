What you are looking at

An analyst asks for the authorized ROE in a black-box settled case. The correct answer is "the settlement did not specify one" — never a number back-solved from the revenue requirement and never the utility's requested figure.

  • This is an answer, not a refusal. The system knows something affirmative: the document is silent. That is a fact with a citation, so it renders as an answer with a citation card.
  • The card's status chip is the third family — ABSENT. Dashed border, no fill, and the words NOT STATED — the document does not state this figure. Never blank, never a dash, never omitted (AC-F36-02).
  • The verbatim quote is the evidence of silence — the settlement's own black-box language. Without it, "not stated" is an assertion; with it, it is a citation.
  • What was requested is shown, clearly labelled as requested, because that is exactly the number an analyst under time pressure would otherwise reach for.

Below the product surface is the side-by-side that motivates all of this: NOT_STATED vs. a parse failure (AC-F14-10 / AC-F14-11). They are different facts and they get different components, different wording and different follow-up.

The settlement did not specify a return on common equity. The PA PUC approved a black-box settlement in Docket R-2021-3024601 on 16 December 2021. The settlement states a total revenue increase of $132.0 million and expressly declines to state the underlying cost-of-capital parameters [1].

For context only: PECO requested 10.35% in its direct testimony in the same docket [2]. That figure was never adopted by the commission and is not evidence of what the settlement embedded.

The distinction that must never collapse · AC-F14-10 vs AC-F14-11

Why two components, not one

"The settlement did not state an ROE" and "we could not read the ROE out of this document" are different facts about the world. Collapsing both into a blank field, a dash, or the word "unknown" destroys the system's ability to refuse honestly — and it is the one place where a well-meaning "N/A" would manufacture a false certainty. So they get different components, different wording, and different next actions.
