The system does not fall back to a keyword search when a question cannot be resolved. Rate-case testimony is highly formulaic — a broad text search across three commissions returns confidently similar prose from unrelated proceedings, which is the single most likely route to a blended, wrong answer.

Add any one of these and the question resolves

  • A commission — PA PUC, PUCT or CPUC.
  • A utility or docket number — e.g. Oncor, or R-2024-3042569.
  • A parameter — e.g. ROE, equity ratio, revenue requirement increase.

Example: "What revenue requirement increase did the PUCT authorize for Oncor in 2025?"
