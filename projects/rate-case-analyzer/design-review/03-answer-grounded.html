What ROE has the PA PUC authorized in fully-projected-future-test-year distribution cases since 2023?

The Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission has authorized a return on common equity in two fully-projected-future-test-year electric distribution base rate cases decided since 1 January 2023.

Authorized ROE in PA PUC fully-projected-future-test-year distribution cases since 2023
UtilityDocketOrder dateAuthorized ROESource
PPL Electric Utilities Corporation R-2024-3042569 19 Dec 2024 9.85% [1]
PECO Energy Company R-2023-3037679 14 Dec 2023 9.60% [2]

Both figures are commission-authorized returns stated in a final order and expressed on a retail jurisdictional basis. In the PECO proceeding the company had requested 10.95% [3]; that figure was requested and not granted, and is shown here only so the spread between the ask and the outcome is visible.

No average, blend or peer benchmark is produced. Each figure is reported against its own named case.
