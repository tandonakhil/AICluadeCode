Variant · the all-cases-stale caveat (AC-F32-02)

Why this is a different component

Above, one of three supporting cases was outside the vintage window, so the caveat is attached to that case. When every supporting case is outside the window, the caveat is about the answer as a whole and is rendered at answer level, naming the oldest and newest supporting order dates. The two must not look the same: "one of these is old" and "all of this is old" are different findings.

All supporting precedent predates the vintage window. Every case supporting this answer was decided more than five years ago — oldest supporting order 16 Mar 2018, newest 09 Nov 2020. Cost of capital is a function of contemporaneous interest rates and market conditions, and figures from before the December 2017 federal tax-rate change were computed under a different statutory rate. Treat these as historical context rather than as current market evidence.
