What you are looking at — and this is the one to look at hardest

This screen exists to be visually incompatible with screen 06. On 06 the system examined forty cases and found none comparable. Here it examined nothing at all. Both produce a refusal. If those two screens looked alike, the product would have a silence-is-clearance failure and every other guarantee would be decorative.

  • The coverage panel does not render a bar. A bar with zero length, or a bar with nothing in it, reads at a glance as "all clean". So the zero case is a different element entirely: a dashed, hatched band that cannot be scanned as a progress bar, headed Nothing was examined, with the filter values that produced the empty set printed inside it.
  • The filter values are the finding. "Checked 0" alone is useless; "checked 0, because you asked for a commission this corpus does not contain" is actionable (AC-F37-03, AC-F31-08).
  • Still neutral, still not an error. Same refusal panel treatment as 06. Asking about a commission outside the corpus is a reasonable thing to do and the tool should not scold you for it.

A side-by-side of the two coverage panels is at the bottom of this page.
