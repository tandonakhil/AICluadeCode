Grid Maintenance Policy (Sample Document)

Scheduled maintenance on regional grid infrastructure occurs during off-peak
hours, defined as 1:00 AM to 5:00 AM local time, to minimize disruption to
consumers and businesses.

Any substation reporting a load above 90% capacity for more than two
consecutive hours must be flagged for priority inspection within 48 hours.

Emergency maintenance (unplanned outages, equipment failure) takes precedence
over scheduled maintenance and may occur at any hour. Affected customers must
be notified within 15 minutes of a known outage exceeding 30 minutes in
expected duration.

All maintenance crews must file a completion report within 24 hours of work
finishing, including load readings before and after the intervention.
