Renewable Incentives FAQ (Sample Document)

Q: What incentive is available for residential solar installations?
A: Eligible households receive a rebate covering 20% of installation cost, up
to a maximum of $4,000, provided the system is installed by a certified
contractor and connected to the grid within 12 months of approval.

Q: Are commercial wind projects eligible for incentives?
A: Commercial wind projects above 1 MW capacity qualify for an accelerated
depreciation schedule and a production tax credit for the first 10 years of
operation.

Q: How long does incentive approval typically take?
A: Residential applications are typically processed within 30 business days.
Commercial applications require an environmental review and typically take
90 to 120 business days.

Q: Can incentives be combined with other regional programs?
A: Yes, these incentives can be stacked with most municipal-level programs
unless explicitly stated otherwise in the municipal program's terms.
